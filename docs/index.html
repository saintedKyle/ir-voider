IR Voider

The offline IR remote for Android phones with a built-in IR blaster.

Phone only · Android 6.0+ · no tracking
Download APK Source on GitHub
Requires a phone with an IR emitter. It does not work on iPhones, iPads, tablets, PCs, or phones without IR hardware. Google Play release is pending.

What it does

33 device types

TV, air conditioner, projector, fan, LED, soundbar, audio, streaming and more.

1,502 remotes

17,789 IR codes from the public Flipper-IRDB database, stored offline.

Auto-find

Sends power codes until your device reacts, with a Previous replay if you miss it.

Saved devices

Name your TV, AC or projector and switch between them.

Works offline

Every remote and scan runs in airplane mode. No ads, no accounts, no analytics — the one optional network call is the update check, and you can switch it off.

Import

Add raw .ir captures as a Learned remote.

How it works

The phone's IR LED sends the same kind of invisible pulses as a normal remote. IR Voider uses Android's ConsumerIrManager to transmit stored timing patterns. There is no Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, account or server involved. IR is line-of-sight: point the top edge at the device.

The phone cannot receive IR, so it cannot learn directly from another remote. Importing a raw capture made with an external IR receiver is supported.
