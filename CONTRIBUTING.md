# Contributing

IR Voider is a **personal project**. External contributions are not accepted.

- Please do **not** open pull requests. They will be closed without review.
- Feature requests and general questions are not accepted through GitHub.
- Bug reports may be closed; the maintainer is the only person who requests
  changes.
- Security issues: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Forks

The GPL-3.0-or-later license legally permits forking. The repository may disable
GitHub's fork button as a courtesy, but that does not remove GPL rights. The
maintainer does not support, review or endorse forks.

Do not publish modified builds under the **IR Voider** name or logo. The branding
is not licensed for forks or redistributed builds.

If you believe you need a change, contact the maintainer directly rather than
opening a PR.
